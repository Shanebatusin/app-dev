# My favorite movie
**Eternal Sunshine of the Spotless Mind**

## Synopsis 
> After a painful breakup, Joel Barish discovers that his ex-girlfriend, Clementine, has undergone a procedure to erase all memories of their relationship. Heartbroken, he decides to do the same. As the memories begin to fade, Joel realizes he still loves her and attempts to preserve his recollections of her before they're lost forever.
